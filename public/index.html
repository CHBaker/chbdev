How I lead

I keep every contract staffed and every engineer knowing where they stand.

  • Led up to 13 engineers, most of the engineering team at one point, through quarterly reviews, 1:1s, and performance interventions, and ran the promotion-readiness reviews.
  • Ran up to five client engagements at the same time, and kept my own work 100% billable on top.
  • Owned staffing and capacity against the contract roadmap. I interviewed candidates and made the hiring decisions.

A small team can ship a lot when the setup is right.

  • Led a three-engineer team that shipped three apps for Shostak, a client startup, in six months. We used AI for design, agentic coding, context files, skills, and pull-request review.
  • By our own sprint measure, the team's velocity roughly doubled after the company's first AI rollout.

Shostak: three apps in six months, inside a capped budget

A streaming platform for independent filmmakers. A Briebug client project. I was the solution engineering lead and the technical lead.

The client wanted to work with us and hesitated on cost. I fit the scope to a negotiated cap and kept the cap visible, so every trade-off could be argued in the open. Sprint 0 meant the team was building product on day one instead of wiring up tools.

For a stretch late in the build, I was the only engineer. I designed the solution for the admin billing system and the creator revenue-share logic, and led the team that built them.

  • Delivered inside the cap, with a larger epic than we first agreed.
  • Three major releases.
  • A launch announced at the Sundance Film Festival.
